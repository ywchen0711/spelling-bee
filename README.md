# 拼字大賽 Spelling Bee

An English spelling bee for the whole family: **https://ywchen0711.github.io/spelling-bee/**

- **10 levels × 40 words**, from kindergarten words (cat, frog) to real winning words of the Scripps National Spelling
  Bee (appoggiatura, cymotrichous, scherenschnitte). Every word has its part of speech, definition, a sentence and its
  language of origin.
- **The pronouncer** speaks with the browser's own voice. Like a real bee you can ask to repeat, say it slowly, or for the
  definition, a sentence, the origin or the part of speech.
- **Contest**: you and four rival spellers on stage, eight rounds. Misses cost a chance (1 = official rules, up to 3);
  survive to win, earn up to three stars and unlock the next level. Tap the stage to hurry the rivals.
- **Practice**: ten words at a time, the ones you have not mastered first; a missed word comes back at the end of the
  session. Each word shows how your spelling differs from the right one and is spelled out loud.
- **Family Bee**: pass the device around; each speller picks their own level, so everyone can play together.
- Word lists to study, a timer per word, voice and speed settings, 繁體中文 / English, phones and computers, cloud saves.

`node tools/test.mjs` checks the word lists.
